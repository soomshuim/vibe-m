## Research Findings

1. 2026 streaming context is hybrid, not just new-release pop.
   - Chart visibility includes new singles, album-event clusters, legacy catalog spikes, viral R&B, and pop-soul records.
   - For Wavvy, this means the rewrite should not chase one narrow current slang surface. It should use familiar, instantly legible pop language with a classic soul/R&B center.

2. Strong current Pop/R&B lyrics use speakable, low-friction lines.
   - The strongest pattern is conversational phrasing that feels like something a vocalist could say out loud.
   - Avoid dense metaphors, abstract thesis lines, and overexplained emotion.
   - Korean application: short everyday clauses, clean verbs, easy mouth-shapes, and images that land quickly.

3. Hook design is phrase-first, not story-first.
   - Current sticky songs often build around a compact repeated phrase or emotional turn that can survive outside the verse story.
   - Repetition works best when the repeated hook is slightly reframed around it, not when the same surrounding line is copied mechanically.
   - For Track 01, keep `올라가` as the hook anchor, but vary nearby images: light, floor, breath, color, steps, air.

4. The emotional engine is simple but internally mixed.
   - Even bright tracks often carry a small contrast: wanting/holding back, ordinary day/sudden lift, confidence/softness.
   - For 17-00, the contrast should be light: low battery to renewed motion, not heartbreak or commute narrative.
   - Use a small before/after curve rather than direct "I am happy" statements.

5. Pop-soul familiarity matters.
   - Successful bright Pop/R&B references classic soul, Motown, doo-wop, retro guitar/organ/bass, or gospel lift while staying modern.
   - Lyric language can mirror that by using tactile, visible nouns: light, color, hand, step, floor, window, air, rhythm.
   - Avoid office/time words because this series treats time as sound positioning, not lyric topic.

## Lexicon Pack

Use:
- motion: 올라가, 가볍게, 한 칸, 발끝, 고개, 돌아, 흔들려, 번져
- brightness/color: 빛, 색, 반짝, 노란, 파란, 선명, 환해져
- body/rhythm: 손끝, 어깨, 숨, 박자, 리듬, 맥박, 발걸음
- space/object: 창, 거리, 바닥, 공기, 스피커, 문, 그림자
- emotion by inference: 웃음이 난다, 표정이 풀린다, 마음이 열린다, 괜찮아진다

Avoid:
- direct time/activity: 17:00, 퇴근, 사무실, 업무, clock-out, commute
- heavy emotion labels: 사랑해, 슬퍼, 외로워, 무너져, 아파
- dark R&B markers: 밤새, 취해, toxic, broken, lonely-room framing
- trend-copy risk: distinctive English catchphrases, famous lyric turns, long repeated borrowed cadences

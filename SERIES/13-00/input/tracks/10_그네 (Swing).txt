=== STYLE ===
Articulation. Silky midtempo Korean R&B, Korean Pop, Like Spring Swing, 110 BPM, E Minor,
warm Rhodes and soft electric piano chords over a relaxed bounce,
light funk guitar chops, round sub-bass lift,
gentle rimshots and brushed snare, intimate groove,
Male and Female duet vocal: trading lines in verses, tight close-mic leads,
Pre-chorus lifts with rising call-and-response,
Chorus blooms with full male and female harmonies in thirds and fifths, airy layered hook,
hook circles like a mantra between two voices,
Bridge builds with emotional swell, unison held note into chorus. Higher register encouraged.
r&b, korean pop

autotune heavy, vocoder, EDM drops, big room, idol group chant,
heavy 808 bass, trap hi-hats, drill bass, dark moody,
aggressive, gritty, distortion, shoegaze wall-of-sound,

jangly reverb bleed, room noise, raw demo quality

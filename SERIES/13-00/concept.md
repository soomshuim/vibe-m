## Series DNA

- **정체성**: 한국어 가사 × Korean Indie Pop + Dream Pop (밝고 두근거리는 봄 사운드)
- **시간대**: 13:00 — 점심 후, 벚꽃길 산책
- **테마**: 봄의 감정들 — 첫사랑, 설렘, 벚꽃, 햇살, 새 시작, 두근거림
- **톤**: 밝고 따뜻하면서 몽환적. 경쾌하지만 과하지 않은 에너지
- **가사**: Suno 자체 작사 (작사 프롬프트 또는 비움)
- **보컬**: Female 55% / Male 45% (F11:M9), Bright & Sweet tone
- **20트랙 / 80분+**

## Style Templates

> 각 템플릿 900자 이하. `STYLE.md` Writing Formula 7요소 + 13:00 특화.
> Harmony Guard + Energy Permission 필수 포함.

### Style A — Korean Indie Pop (메인)

```
Articulation. Korean Indie Pop, {BPM} BPM, {Key},
bright jangly electric guitar riff, clean tone with light chorus effect,
upbeat drum pattern, crisp snare, open hi-hat,
warm bass groove, subtle synth pad layer,
{Gender} vocal: Bright, Sweet, Clear, Chest voice, articulation,
catchy melodic hook, singalong chorus,
warm bright mix, wide stereo, spring energy,
moderate reverb, open room ambience, EQ balanced sound, clean mix.
Lead vocal remains single and dominant throughout. No stacked or choir-like harmonies.
Vocal line may intensify dynamically (belt, higher register), but no additional vocal layers.
Chorus vocal may be delivered with stronger intensity or light belt.
Higher register emphasis is encouraged. Natural vocal brightness allowed.
```

### Style B — Spring Dream Pop

```
Articulation. Korean Dream Pop, {BPM} BPM, {Key},
shimmering reverb guitar, lush ethereal pad, soft glockenspiel accent,
gentle drum with brush snare, soft kick, light tambourine,
warm deep bass, dreamy synth texture,
{Gender} vocal: Airy, Sweet, Gentle, Chest voice, articulation,
floating melodic contour, sustained chorus hook,
dreamy atmospheric mix, wide stereo, lush reverb space,
heavy reverb, ethereal ambience, EQ balanced sound, clean mix.
Lead vocal remains single and dominant throughout. No stacked or choir-like harmonies.
Vocal line may intensify dynamically (gentle lift, higher register), but no additional vocal layers.
Chorus vocal may be delivered with tender intensity or gentle sustained note.
Higher register emphasis is allowed. Natural vocal warmth encouraged.
```

### Style C — Dreamy Indie Pop (혼합)

```
Articulation. Korean Indie Pop, Dream Pop, {BPM} BPM, {Key},
jangly electric guitar with shimmer reverb, bright piano chords,
upbeat drum with soft snare, tambourine, light strings in chorus,
warm bass groove, ethereal synth pad layer,
{Gender} vocal: Bright, Airy, Sweet, Chest voice, articulation,
catchy hook with dreamy atmosphere, melodic chorus,
warm dreamy mix, wide stereo, spring warmth with ethereal space,
moderate-heavy reverb, open ambience, EQ balanced sound, clean mix.
Lead vocal remains single and dominant throughout. No stacked or choir-like harmonies.
Vocal line may intensify dynamically (belt, higher register), but no additional vocal layers.
Chorus vocal may be delivered with stronger intensity or light belt.
Higher register emphasis is encouraged. Natural vocal brightness allowed.
```

### Exclude Template (공통)

```
autotune heavy, vocoder, EDM drops, big room,
choir, stacked harmonies, backing vocal layers, doubled vocals,
heavy 808 bass, trap hi-hats, drill bass, dark moody,
aggressive, gritty, distortion, shoegaze wall-of-sound
```
